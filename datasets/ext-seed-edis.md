## Overview

东南亚多族群随访；脑部微出血和眼底/认知的可验证分析子集。

当前核验的影像与模态范围 — Fundus \| OCT/OCTA (selected waves) \| brain MRI (EDIS)

Image availability — SEED has dataset/image proposal forms. Request EDIS ancillary MRI/retinal linkage separately; approval of SEED images alone does not authorize EDIS ancillary data.

## Research value and experimental design

| Field | Recorded value |
| --- | --- |
| Individual linkage / 个体配对依据 | EDIS study enrolled older SCES participants, examined retina, brain MRI, neuropsychology in same participants. DeepRETStroke used SEED for incident stroke in a different sample. Do not generalize EDIS pairing to the parent. |
| Strengths / 主要优点 | 东南亚多族群随访；脑部微出血和眼底/认知的可验证分析子集。 |
