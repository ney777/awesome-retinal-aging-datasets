| Field | Recorded value |
| --- | --- |
| Individual linkage / 个体配对依据 | 1936-born cohort wave 2 (~age 73), bilateral fundus vascular measures and structural MRI. Subsequent cognitive scores link to the same people. Do not transfer stroke patients or counts from companion Mild Stroke Study. |
| Strengths / 主要优点 | Narrow age range controls age confounding; measured childhood cognitive ability and later-life repeated tests, MRI small-vessel phenotypes. |
