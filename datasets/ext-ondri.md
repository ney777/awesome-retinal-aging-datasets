## Overview

跨疾病、纵向、多实验室；可先用受控的层厚和认知指标检验假设。

当前核验的影像与模态范围 — Fundus \| SD-OCT \| brain MRI

Image availability — Raw neuroimaging plus tabular OCT retinal thickness are documented. Raw retinal OCT/fundus pixels are not confirmed in accessible release; ask Brain-CODE.
